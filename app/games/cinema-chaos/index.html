📖 HOW TO PLAY

1. Your Job at The Grand Cinema

You work at the ticket inspection booth. Listen to each customer, inspect their ticket against the Cinema Schedule and Manager's Rules, and stamp your decision:

  • ✅ ADMIT: Everything on the ticket matches the schedule, active policies, and the customer's booking.
  • 🛠️ CORRECT: The customer has a fixable mistake (wrong film genre for what they asked, moved screen, or wrong time). Click CORRECT to open the Ticket Printer, pick the right screening, and print a replacement ticket!
  • 🚫 REFUSE: The customer has no ticket, a duplicate serial number, arrives more than 10 minutes late, holds a non-changeable Promo ticket with an error, or their screening is completely sold out with no valid alternative.
  • 💬 QUESTION: If information is missing or subjective (e.g. "I'm going to see a film to relax" with a Thriller ticket), ask a clarifying question! Remember: subjective preferences do not make a valid ticket invalid if the customer confirms they want to watch it.

2. Using the 🔍 Inspect Tool

When INSPECT MODE is ON, click any two items — such as the customer's speech bubble, a field on the ticket, a row in the Cinema Schedule, the booking arrival time, or a Manager Rule — to visually compare them and spot discrepancies.

3. Take your time

You have a countdown to process the shift’s customers. Reading the Help or ID card and reading the decision feedback pause the timer. Late-arrival cases use the fixed arrival time printed on the booking record, not the countdown. After deciding, press Next customer.

Keyboard: A = admit · C = correct · R = refuse · Q = ask which film · I = inspect · P = pause · N = next.

Free seats do not include bookings. A customer with a booking can enter a full screening. A replacement moves their booking; it does not charge for seats twice.
