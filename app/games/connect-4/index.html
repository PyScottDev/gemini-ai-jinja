    How to play

    Connect four counters horizontally, vertically or diagonally to win.

    Choose a verb and give its past simple or past participle, as selected above the board. A correct answer lets you choose a column.

    Classroom: say the answer aloud. The teacher reveals it and marks it correct or incorrect.

    Solo / Two players: type your answer and press Enter or Check. With retries enabled, you can try again. After two mistakes, you may reveal the answer and lose your turn. With one attempt, a wrong answer ends your turn.

    Changing the verb form during a turn takes effect next turn. Counters already placed keep their original words.
